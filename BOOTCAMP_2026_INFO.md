## 📅 Workshop Projects & Environments

### 1. Tabular RL & Discrete MDPs
* **Objective:** Foundational reinforcement learning. Formulating discrete Markov Decision Processes (MDPs) and solving them using Q-Learning and SARSA.
* **Environment:** Interactive `Maze` in a Google Colab notebook.
* **Key Files:** 
  * `Maze environment.ipynb` (Starter template)
  * `Maze solutions.ipynb` (Full solutions with Q-Learning and SARSA)
* **Path in Repo:** `/Tutorial Hands-On Session: Tabular RL & Discrete MDPs` on branch `tutorial-code`.

### 2. Continuous Control & The Reality Gap
* **Objective:** Action policies in continuous spaces. Training continuous control policies in simulation and evaluating policy transfer to altered physics configurations.
* **Environment:** `MuJoCo Ant` $\rightarrow$ `Crippled Ant`.

### 3. Custom MDP Design & Competition
* **Objective:** Design custom reward functions, observation spaces, and transition dynamics to optimize agents for air traffic control, culminating in a group tournament.
* **Environment:** `UMFlightEnv` (Air Traffic Control Tournament).
